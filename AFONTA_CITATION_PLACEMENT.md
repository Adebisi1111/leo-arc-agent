# How & Where to Use the Academic Texts — Afonta Draft

Your family books cover the BIOGRAPHY (his life, the 1978 founding, his works). The ACADEMIC texts do three jobs:
- **CONTEXT** — prove the wider claims about Islam in Yorubaland/Osogbo (Background §1–2)
- **GAP EVIDENCE** — prove the field studies the *tradition* but not *him* (Background §4, Problem §1–3, §5)
- **METHOD** — justify oral history (Problem §5 / method)

## BACKGROUND TO THE STUDY

### §1 — "spread of Islam in Yorubaland… scholars established Qur'anic schools…"
**Use as CONTEXT.** Weave in:
> "The spread of Islam in Yorubaland has been closely associated with the activities of Islamic scholars who established Qur'anic schools, Arabic institutions, and centres of learning (Gbadamosi, 1978; Peel, 2000; Reichmuth, 1998)."
- Gbadamosi (1978) = classic survey of Islam among the Yoruba.
- Peel (2000) = Yoruba religious history frame.
- Reichmuth (1998) = Ilorin as a learning centre (sets up his training later).

### §2 — "Osogbo… notable centre of Islamic scholarship… growth sustained by scholars"
**Use as CONTEXT (Osogbo-specific).**
> "Although Osogbo is widely known for the Osun-Osogbo Festival, it also developed into a centre of Islamic scholarship, sustained by scholars who established Qur'anic schools and Arabic institutions (Folorunsho, 1996; Rauf & Fahm, 2022; Olugunna, 1987)."
- Folorunsho (1996) = the key Osogbo Muslim scholarship article. **Anchor citation.**
- Rauf & Fahm (2022) = recent Osogbo Islam study (your corrected "Fahm 2022").
- Olugunna (1987) = *History of Osogbo* — town background.

### §3 — "Born in Ilorin, 1953… Ilorin tradition… relocated to Osogbo… 1978 founded Mahad"
**Split: academic for the TRAINING CONTEXT, family + news for the FACTS.**
> "Born in Ilorin in 1953, he received his early Islamic education within the renowned Ilorin tradition of Arabic and Islamic scholarship (Reichmuth, 1998; Jamiu, 2014), before relocating to Osogbo where, in 1978, he established Mahad Zumratu Soliheena (Banni, 2024)."
- Reichmuth (1998) + Jamiu (2014) = Ilorin/Al-Iluri scholarly tradition (CONTEXT for "where he trained").
- Banni (2024) = family book for the 1978 founding + biographical facts.
- ADD for the factual claims (born Ilorin 1953, moved to Osogbo, died 2023): National Insight (2023), OsunDefender (2023) — these are PRIMARY news, cite alongside family.
> "…and devoted over four decades to Islamic education until his death in 2023 (National Insight, 2023; OsunDefender, 2023; Banni, 2024)."

### §4 — "Despite his prominence… little attention in academic historical studies"
**Use as GAP EVIDENCE.** This is where you TURN the academic sources against themselves:
> "Despite his prominence, Sheikh Abdul-Wahab Banni has received little attention in academic historical studies. While works such as Folorunsho (1996) and Rauf and Fahm (2022) examine Islam in Osogbo collectively, and Gbadamosi (1978) and Reichmuth (1998) treat the broader Yoruba/Ilorin tradition, none offers a study of the individual scholar. Even where Osogbo scholars have been profiled — as in Olugunna's (1990) biography of Alhaji Ashiru Uthman — Afonta is absent from the record."
- Folorunsho / Rauf & Fahm / Gbadamosi / Reichmuth = show the field is TRADITION-LEVEL, not person-level.
- Olugunna (1990) = the CONTRAST proof — another Osogbo scholar got a biography, Afonta didn't. **This is your strongest gap sentence.**

### §5 — "It is against this background that this study examines…"
**Use as SCOPE anchor (Osun historiography).**
> "…this study contributes to the historiography of Islam in Osun State (Oyeweso, 2012; Makinde, 2007)."
- Oyeweso (2012) + Makinde (2007) = Osun State Islamic/social history frame.

## STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM

### §1 — "history of Islam… received considerable scholarly attention… Ilorin, Ibadan, Lagos"
**Use as CONTEXT (what EXISTS).**
> "Existing studies have examined the spread of Islam among the Yoruba (Gbadamosi, 1978), the Ilorin scholarly tradition (Reichmuth, 1998; Jamiu, 2014), and Muslim scholars in major centres (Peel, 2000)."
- These prove the field is rich — but centred on big names/centres.

### §2 — "studies on Osogbo… Arabic literary activities… socio-cultural environment"
**Use as CONTEXT (Osogbo exists too).**
> "Studies on Osogbo have explored the growth of Islam and Arabic literary activity (Folorunsho, 1996; Rauf & Fahm, 2022; Olugunna, 1987)."
- Shows Osogbo IS studied — but at the collective level.

### §3 — "little scholarly attention… Sheikh Abdul-Wahab Banni"
**Use as GAP (the pivot).**
> "However, despite these contributions, little scholarly attention has been devoted to the life and contributions of Sheikh Abdul-Wahab Banni. Where individual Osogbo scholars have been profiled, as with Olugunna's (1990) study of Ashiru Uthman, Afonta remains undocumented."
- Olugunna (1990) again = the contrast that makes the gap concrete.

### §4 — "established Mahad Zumratu Soliheena in 1978… four decades…"
**Family + news for facts; flag the year.**
> "Sheikh Abdul-Wahab Banni established Mahad Zumratu Soliheena in 1978 and spent over four decades promoting Islamic education (Banni, 2024; National Insight, 2023)."
- Banni (2024) = family source for 1978. **FLAG: no independent source confirms 1978** → either soften to "late 1970s" or find a dated record.

### §5 — "available information… commemorative publications, sermons, newspaper reports, family… descriptive and devotional"
**Use as GAP-METHOD bridge.** Cite the actual non-academic sources, then contrast with academic:
> "Most available information consists of commemorative publications, sermons, and newspaper reports (OsunDefender, 2023; National Insight, 2023; Voice Air Media, 2023; Banni, 2024). While valuable, these are largely descriptive and devotional and do not offer critical historical analysis — unlike the scholarly treatments of Osogbo Islam by Folorunsho (1996) and Rauf and Fahm (2022)."
- News = OsunDefender, National Insight, Voice Air (2023).
- Family = Banni (2024).
- Contrast = Folorunsho / Rauf & Fahm (academic).
- **Add method justification:** "This study therefore adopts oral-history and archival methods appropriate to recovering under-documented religious biographies (Na'Allah, 2020)."
  - Na'Allah (2020) = justifies your approach as academically legitimate.

### §6 — "this study seeks to fill this gap… historiography of Islam in Osun State"
**Use as SIGNIFICANCE anchor.**
> "This study fills this gap and contributes to the historiography of Islam in Osun State (Oyeweso, 2012; Makinde, 2007)."
- Same Osun frame as Background §5.

Concord

Autonomous Payroll & Subscription Agent · Arc Testnet × Circle

Problem

  • Recurring payments (salaries, subscriptions) still need a human to click "pay" every cycle.
  • No transparent, on-chain record of who got paid and when.

Solution

  • Concord is an AI agent that monitors due dates, funds a SubscriptionVault, and executes USDC payments autonomously.
  • Powered by Arc (Circle's L1) + Circle Programmable Payments.
  • Zero manual approval per cycle — the agent runs on a 5-minute cron.

How it works

  • 1. Detects subscriptions due in the vault
  • 2. Tops up vault from owner wallet if balance is low
  • 3. Calls vault.pay() on-chain for each due sub
  • 4. Logs the settled tx + confirms completion

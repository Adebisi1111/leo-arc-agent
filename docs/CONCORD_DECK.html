Concord

An autonomous payroll agent you built — it pays recurring USDC on Arc, on schedule, with no human in the loop.

Concord is a boss who never forgets payday. It's an agent that runs real payroll on a blockchain — and a normal person can watch it work on a plain dashboard.

What it is (the agent, not the plumbing)

An agent with its own wallet + its own contract. Concord holds a USDC wallet and deployed its own SubscriptionVault that stores payment schedules and settles payroll on-chain.

It enforces who can pay (agent or owner only) and emits a Paid event on every payout — verifiable by anyone on ArcScan.

It runs hands-off. Schedules fire on their own; when a payment is due, the agent calls vault.pay() and USDC lands with no manual approval per cycle.

A human can watch it. A simple dashboard shows live payments — no wallets, no gas, no contract jargon.

How it works — the agent's loop

1 · Check. On schedule, Concord reads due dates from its SubscriptionVault.
2 · Pay. Agent calls vault.pay() → USDC settles to the recipient on Arc.
3 · Prove. A Paid event fires on-chain; the dashboard reads it live.
4 · Repeat. A daily anchor guarantees ≥1 real on-chain tx/day — fully autonomous, no human in the loop.

Plumbing note: execution is routed through Circle's Smart Contract Platform to bypass Arc's public-RPC throttle. That's infrastructure — the agent is the product.

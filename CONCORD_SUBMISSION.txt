PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Concord is an autonomous USDC payroll agent built natively on Arc Testnet. It automates recurring salary payments by monitoring payment schedules, executing transactions on chain through the SubscriptionVault smart contract using Circle programmable payments, and confirming completed payments without requiring manual approval for each payment cycle. Powered by Arc and Circle infrastructure, Concord demonstrates a practical Agentic Economy use case where AI agents can securely manage recurring financial operations in a transparent and reliable way.
